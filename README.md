## 🌿 About the lesson

This lesson introduces broad-scale biodiversity and richness patterns and shows how ecological hypotheses can be compared with data. Participants work with diversity measures, species-richness patterns, model comparison, and the consequences of sampling and spatial structure.

## 🎯 Learning goals

By the end of the lesson, participants should be able to:

- distinguish species richness from other measures of biological diversity;
- describe spatial diversity patterns and formulate competing ecological explanations;
- compare hypotheses using statistical models and information criteria;
- recognize how sampling design and scale influence apparent richness patterns.
